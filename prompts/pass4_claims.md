You are given the introduction and background/related-work sections. Extract every sentence that presents a fact as established knowledge and would need an external source to confirm: constants, dates, statistics, market or population figures, "it is well known that...", "prior work has shown...", attributions of a result to another author.

For each claim give the exact quotation (<= 40 words), its location, and a short web-search query that would find an authoritative source for it. Do not include claims about the paper's own results or methods. Do not include vague motivational statements with nothing checkable in them.

Also give a search query that would find major published work on the paper's main research question, in `main_result_query`.
